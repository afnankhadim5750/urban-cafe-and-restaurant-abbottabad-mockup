The Café · Jinnahabad

A modern room in the middle of the plaza.

Urban Cafe & Restaurant sits in Jadoon Plaza Phase II, the shopping complex at the centre of Jinnahabad — the stop locals and visitors make between the shops, after a day out in the hills, or for a long evening with friends.

Chapter One · Coffee

Slow coffee, poured properly.

The café half of the room runs on espresso: cappuccinos, lattes and the barista's pour, served hot in real cups — the kind of coffee that turns a shopping break into an hour.

  1. The shotEspresso pulled fresh for every cup — never a pot left standing.
  2. The milkSteamed silky, not scalded, so the coffee stays smooth to the last sip.
  3. The pourFinished with latte art and served hot, at the table or at the counter.

Chapter Three · Fast Food

When the table is hungry, order fast food.

Burgers, crispy chicken, loaded fries and chicken bites — cooked to order and served hot, built for groups that want to eat with their hands and talk with their mouths full.

Chapter Four · Pasta & Sweet Finish

Creamy pasta, then something sweet.

The last chapter of the menu: fettuccine Alfredo — plain or with grilled chicken — and a tall slice of chocolate layer cake to close the table. Order it as a main, or as the second course after the wok.

Good To Know

Questions, answered.

Where exactly is Urban Cafe & Restaurant?

Inside Jadoon Plaza Phase II on Jadoon Plaza Phase 2 Road, Jinnahabad, Mandian, Abbottabad — in the shopping complex itself, so you can combine a visit with the plaza's shops.

What kind of food do you serve?

Chinese, fast food, coffee and pasta — chow mein and Manchurian, burgers and fries, cappuccinos and lattes, Alfredo pasta and chocolate cake. Ask in store for today's full menu.

What are the opening hours?

Daily 12:00 PM to 12:00 AM, with a later Friday opening at 2:00 PM. Hours are as listed on Google — call ahead to confirm.

Is it good for groups and families?

Yes — the room is built for it: shared dishes in the middle of the table, cozy seating, and a menu with something for every appetite.
